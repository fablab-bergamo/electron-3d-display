Three rules to watch

Aufbau: use the usual subshell order as a starting point: 1s → 2s → 2p → 3s → 3p → 4s → 3d → 4p. Some elements have different ground-state occupancies.
Hund: within a subshell, occupy the equal-energy orbitals singly with parallel spins before pairing.
Pauli: an orbital holds at most two electrons, with opposite spins.
